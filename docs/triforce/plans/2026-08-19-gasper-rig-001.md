### 3. Front-only cage (two regimes)

Workers: `n̂·V`, `z<0`, and “inside `#body`” are **three different objects**. Only the canal has a back.

**Regime A — rest loft (`fabricLive === false`)**  
There is no back. A graph that “hides the far side” is a card-fold and pizzas the W.

```
visible(i) := SHOW_GRID && π(P_i) ∈ clip(#body)
```

Do not apply `n̂·V`. Do not apply `z<0`. Rest film after R2 must still show the **full W grid**.

**Regime B — canal ready** (E3 interiors + E4 plant-yaw + **nz-flip deleted**)

```
visible(i) :=
    n̂_i · V ≥ −ε
AND π(P_i) ∈ Ω                 // occupied solid, not even-odd on the inked path
AND frontmost(π(P_i), i)       // far foot dies interior to Ω, not z<0
```

`n̂ = ∂P/∂u × ∂P/∂v` from stored xyz. `V = (0,0,1)` after `rotateAboutM`. Hertzmann–Zorin `{n̂·V=0}` is a **curve check**, never the hide mask.

**Front** pill = limit-to-visible (default). **X-ray** pill = paint all, dim hidden, pick may hit backs. Do not couple them.

Pick skips `¬visible` unless x-ray. Soft falloff is geodesic in `(r, Δs)`, never screen-projected (leaks the W cleft).

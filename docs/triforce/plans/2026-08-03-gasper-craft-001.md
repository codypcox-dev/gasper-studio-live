## 3. The five subsystems

### C1 — CurveTrack authority (the graph editor) · canon: `keyframe-evaluate`, `bezier-tangent-model`, `arc-phase-law`, `anticipation-dip`, `follow-through-settle`
New `packages/desktop/src/gasper/curves/`:
- `CurveTrack.ts` (pure, frozen): keys `(t, v)`; per-key tangent type closed enum `stepped | linear | spline-auto | flat-clamped | overshoot`; tangent weights; deterministic cubic/Hermite evaluation + first derivative (spacing). Tests: tangent semantics (stepped holds exact; linear constant slope; flat zero-slope at key; C1 continuity), determinism, derivative spacing.
- `PerformancePack.ts`: multi-channel synchronized tracks (`world_x`, `world_y`, `tilt`, `squash`, `stretch`, `wake`, `camera_scale`, `camera_x/y`, `light`, `face`) + **beat sheet** (C5) + per-segment mode map (authored/physics) + hash + provenance tag.
- `arcPhaseValidator`: for any `(world_x, world_y)` authored pair, require quarter-period phase offset between velocity peaks; reject aligned peaks unless the segment is flagged `mechanical` (`arc-phase-law`).
- `squashVolumeGuard`: post-exaggeration `|Sx*Sy − 1| ≤ 0.02` (`squash-stretch-volume-guard`).
- Intake: `GasperRigController` new `runPerformancePack(pack, {provenance})` → new `PerformancePackDriver` on the organism clock (same transport idiom as WorldPhysicsDriver, priority 26); forwards world pose through the Phase A fence as `curve-authority`, silhouette deltas through the D-0090 fence, wake/light feeds, and (new) camera + face intents. Auto-release at pack end (settled-hold forward carries the last authored values, D-0018 idiom).
- Authored packs as data: `packages/desktop/src/gasper/curves/packs/` — `s2-bounce-three-beat.json`, `s4-comet-whip.json` first (the two performances the owner just rejected, re-authored as craft).

### C2 — ShotDirector (camera grammar) · canon: `staging`, `shot-scales`, `layout-first-composition`
- `ShotDirector.ts`: per-beat **shot scale** from the pack's beat sheet (closed `shot-scales` enum with body-fraction bands). Computes camera zoom/pan so the body's bbox occupies the band; reframes on beat boundaries with a fast ease (0.25s) — a cut language without raster flashes.
- Default performance scales: `medium`/`wide`. `extreme-wide` forbidden during performance beats (validator).
- Ownership rule (D-0089 idiom): while a pack runs, ShotDirector owns the camera; user pan/zoom or follow-release overrides and disarms direction; home return eases back then releases.
- Staging gate in captures: measured body-frame fraction inside the declared band on every beat shot.

### C3 — Stage world (void → place) · canon: `vector-25d-render`, `multiplane-depth`, `light-as-material`
- Vector environment in the SVG stage (no raster): horizon gradient + far depth band, floor plane with sparse vector contour lines receding with the depth lanes, two parallax mid-bands (`multiplane-depth`: layer rate monotonic with depth under camera travel), a soft light pool under the body that tracks world x and altitude (floor-pinned, altitude-attenuated like the shadow), vignette.
- Material-as-light: the body's existing interior light (`MOTION_LIGHT`, presence glow) casts into the light pool and horizon tint — light response reads as material identity; energy-conservative (pool intensity bounded, falls off with altitude).
- All layers static-safe under reduced motion (environment persists; only motion-coupled light gated by motionStrength). Home byte-stability preserved: environment is additive art, worldRig transform still absent at home.

### C4 — Exaggeration & readability · canon: `exaggeration`, `squash-stretch-volume-guard`, `facs-expression-budget`, `secondary-action`
- Rail group **"Craft"**: `Exaggeration` (0.5–2, default 1.25) multiplies squash/stretch/wake/tilt amplitudes **reciprocally** (volume law enforced after scaling); `Shot bias` (medium↔wide); `Tempo` (0.75–1.25 pack time scale).
- Screen-space amplitude floor: packs authored so bounce apex ≥ 40% frame height at their shot scale; comet traverse ≥ 80% of stage width on screen (the owner's bar: "whips across the screen").
- Face beats as secondary action: excitement widen on gather/launch, blink + squash-coincident eye shut on impact, settle smile — authored on the `face` channel with AU-named intents (AU6+AU12 for the settle smile), onset budgets per `facs-expression-budget`.

### C5 — Beat authoring & review gates · canon: `animatic-beat-sheet`, `limited-animation-hold`, `review-gate-phases`, `timing`
- Every pack ships its **beat sheet**: `beats: [{id, t0, t1, primaryIdea, shotScale, valueTurn}]` — beginning/middle/end with a value turn (McKee), one primary idea per beat.
- The demo/loop sequences compose from beats; **no void time**: idle stretches become authored holds on 2s stepping with composition (limited-animation economy) — the dormant-torus void and 40s idle stretches are replaced by held, composed beats.
- Capture drive re-proofed per beat (telemetry-synced shot per beat boundary + apex/impact moments); gates: staging band, arc phase, volume law, amplitude floors, collapse probe, fence probe.
- Review-gate idiom for our own work: blocking (stepped keys, timings) reviewed in captures before spline polish — we eat our own medicine.
